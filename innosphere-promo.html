Help and answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build our website?

Most school and college websites go live in three to six weeks, depending on the number of pages and modules. We share a clear timeline at the start.

Can our staff update the website themselves?

Yes. You get an easy dashboard to post notices, photos and events, and we train your staff to use it. Our team can also make changes for you.

How does the GPS attendance work?

Students mark attendance from their phone, and the portal accepts it only when they are within a set distance of your campus. Teachers see the records instantly.

Can parents pay fees online?

Yes. We connect a payment gateway so parents can pay by UPI, card or net banking, with automatic receipts and reminders.

Do you provide hosting and support after launch?

Yes. Packages include secure hosting, SSL and backups, and our support team handles updates and changes after launch.
